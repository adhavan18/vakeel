## Description
On 20 June 2025, a government committee approved a 1,320 MW coal plant expansion whose public hearing was waived. It's on page 18 of a 246-page document nobody reads.

Vakeel reads India's environmental clearance filings for the people who live next to the project:
• Amazon Bedrock agents read every project in the filing in parallel, orchestrated by AWS Step Functions
• Every number is verified word for word against the page it came from
• Plain-language findings in English, Hindi and the state's language, with impact computed by code
• A formal objection drafted from the committee's own record, which can't be filed until a real person signs it
