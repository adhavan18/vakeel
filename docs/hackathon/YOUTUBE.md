## Title
Vakeel in 160 seconds: AI agents on AWS read 246 pages of coal plant approvals in 52 seconds

## Description
A 1,320 MW coal plant expansion on the Hasdeo River skipped its public hearing. It's on page 18 of a 246-page government filing. On page 25, it was approved. Nobody objected, because nobody could read it.

Vakeel reads India's environmental clearance filings for the people who live next door:
• Read: AWS Step Functions fans out one Amazon Bedrock (Nova 2 Lite) agent per project, in parallel. 246 pages, 52 seconds.
• Verify: every number needs a word-for-word quote and a page; code checks it against the PDF. 219 claims kept, 65 thrown out.
• Explain: plain language in English, Hindi and Odia; the climate, water and coal math is done by code, not the model.
• Act: a formal objection drafted from the committee's own record. Step Functions waits (waitForTaskToken) until a real person signs.
